Your safety is our priority. This page describes how to report problematic content or behaviour, what we do with those reports, and where to find help when something goes beyond the Platform.

## How to report

Every profile, message and channel has a **Report** option in its menu. We ask you to choose a category and, optionally, add context. The more information you give us, the faster we can act.

Available categories:

- Spam or scam.
- Harassment or threats.
- Impersonation.
- Inappropriate content for a public surface.
- **Suspected minor.**
- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).**
- **Non-consensual intimate images (NCII) — Ley Olimpia.**
- **Digital gender-based violence.**
- Other.

CSAM and NCII reports take absolute priority and are processed outside the normal queue.

## What happens after a report

1. Your report enters the system and you can keep using Circl normally.
2. A member of the moderation team reviews it as soon as possible.
3. If the conduct or content breaches our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) we take the appropriate action: warning, content removal, temporary or permanent suspension.
4. For CSAM, NCII or serious risk we also involve the authorities.
5. We do not notify you individually when each report is resolved, but the reported person never sees who filed the report.

## If you are being extorted with intimate images (sextortion)

Sexual extortion is a crime in Argentina, and **Law 27.736 ("Ley Olimpia")** explicitly covers it as digital gender-based violence.

**What to do now**:

1. **Do not pay**. Paying does not stop the extortioner; it usually escalates the demands.
2. **Save evidence**: screenshots, usernames, URLs, conversations. Do not delete the messages on Circl — we can cooperate with the authorities when there is a legal request.
3. **Report the account inside Circl** with the "Non-consensual intimate images (NCII) – Ley Olimpia" category.
4. **Block** the person from their profile to prevent further contact.
5. **Find help outside the Platform**:
   - **Línea 144** — Argentina's national hotline for women and gender-diverse people facing violence, including digital violence. Available 24/7 across the country. Free and confidential.
   - **911** — Emergencies.
   - **Public Prosecutor's Office in your jurisdiction** — to file a formal report.

If the person extorting you holds intimate images of you that are circulating or might circulate, you can upload them yourself to **[StopNCII.org](https://stopncii.org)** so that a perceptual hash is generated; participating platforms (including Circl, once integration is complete) will block uploads that match.

## Block or mute

From any user's profile you can:

- **Block**: the other person can no longer see you, message you, or find you in searches. It is bidirectional. You can unblock from Settings → Blocked users.
- **Report**: in addition to blocking, you create a record for the moderation team to review.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account**. There is a 30-day grace period during which you can change your mind: signing in again reactivates the account. After that, your data is anonymised or deleted as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Appeals

If your account is suspended and you believe there was an error, you will receive an email with a link to file an appeal. The team reviews each case individually.
