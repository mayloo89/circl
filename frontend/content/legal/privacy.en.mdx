## 1. Who is responsible

Circl is the data controller for the personal data processed on the Platform under **Argentine Personal Data Protection Law 25.326** and its implementing regulations. For any question or to exercise your rights, write to **info.circl.ar@gmail.com**.

Before public launch we will register our personal data databases with the **Agency of Access to Public Information (AAIP)** as required by Law 25.326.

## 2. What we collect

**Account data**

- Email address, password (stored as a bcrypt hash), date of birth.
- Confirmation that you accepted the Terms and this Policy, with timestamp and version.

**Profile data**

- Username, display name, bio, gender, interests, preferred language.
- Photos you choose to upload.
- The approximate location and coordinates you allow us to share for proximity filters.

**Usage data**

- Messages you send (including content), contacts, rooms and channels you take part in, presence indicators, read receipts.
- Moderation reports created by you or about your account.

**Technical data**

- IP address, user agent, push-notification device identifiers.
- Activity logs and operational metrics (see section 9).

## 3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of:

- Your **explicit consent** at registration (Law 25.326, art. 5).
- The **performance of the contract** that arises from your use of the Platform.
- **Compliance with legal obligations** — for example, retention for security or judicial reasons.
- Our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Platform safe and free from abuse, balanced against your rights.

## 4. How we use your data

- To create and manage your account.
- To provide the service's features (search, messaging, presence, notifications).
- To detect and prevent abuse: **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** detection, **non-consensual intimate image (NCII)** matching, moderation of reports.
- To communicate with you about the service (email verification, security alerts, changes to these documents).
- To comply with legal and judicial requirements.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share strictly necessary information with:

- **Infrastructure providers** (hosting, file storage, database, email delivery) acting as data processors on our behalf.
- **CSAM and NCII detection services** (such as PhotoDNA / Cloudflare CSAM Scanning, StopNCII.org) when needed to comply with our legal obligations and to protect users.
- **Competent authorities** when there is a valid court order or applicable legal obligation in the Argentine Republic.

Wherever possible we require our providers to provide guarantees equivalent to those of Law 25.326.

## 6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, personal data is anonymised or deleted after the 30-day grace period, with the following exceptions:

- Messages already delivered to other users (they remain in the recipient's inbox with the sender shown as "deleted user").
- Information needed to comply with legal obligations (for example, records of serious incidents).
- Encrypted backups that are rotated periodically.

## 7. Your rights (Habeas Data)

The Argentine Constitution (article 43) and Law 25.326 grant you the rights of **access, rectification, update, erasure and confidentiality** of your personal data.

You can exercise these rights:

- Directly from the Platform: **Settings → Data → Export my data / Delete my account** (the export will be delivered as a downloadable file once that feature is available).
- By emailing **info.circl.ar@gmail.com** stating which right you wish to exercise and proving your identity. We will respond within the deadlines set by Law 25.326.

You may also file a complaint with the **AAIP** ([www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip)).

## 8. International transfers

Some of our infrastructure providers may store data in countries that are not on the AAIP's list of "adequate countries". When that happens we contractually require a level of protection equivalent to Argentine law.

## 9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data: in-transit encryption (HTTPS/WSS), bcrypt-hashed passwords, restricted access for authorised staff, audit logging. No measure is foolproof — if we detect a data breach that seriously affects your rights we will notify you in time and report to the AAIP where required.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) and to remember your language preference. We do not use advertising tracking cookies.

## 11. Minors

Circl is forbidden to people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that an account belongs to a minor we delete it immediately and, where applicable, report it to the authorities.

## 12. Changes to this policy

When we update this Policy we will notify you on the Platform. If the changes are substantial we will ask you to accept the new version before continuing.

For any question about your data: **info.circl.ar@gmail.com**.
