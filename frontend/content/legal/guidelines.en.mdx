Circl is a community for adults. These Guidelines exist to keep the space safe, respectful and useful for everyone. Accepting them is part of registration and they apply to every interaction on the Platform.

## Minimum age: 18

Circl is for adults only. If we discover that an account belongs to a minor we delete it immediately and, where applicable, notify the authorities.

## Three-tier content model

Different surfaces of the Platform allow different kinds of content. This is intentional: we want consensual adult expression to be possible without intimate content being seen accidentally or without permission.

### Tier 1 — Public profile and channels

Your profile photo, public gallery and open channels are surfaces **without sexually explicit content**. Lingerie, swimwear and underwear are allowed; full nudity and sexual activity are not. Every uploaded image is checked by an automatic classifier before it goes live.

### Tier 2 — Direct messages

Direct messages only open between people who have mutually accepted to connect. In that private context, **sexual content between consenting adults is allowed**. All images still go through our automatic CSAM and NCII filters (see "Hard rules" below).

### Tier 3 — Private albums *(coming soon)*

Soon you will be able to create private albums and grant access to specific people. Each grant is logged so that consent — both yours and the recipient's — is verifiable. This is the recommended surface for sharing intimate content.

## Hard rules

Some conduct and content is forbidden **on every surface, with no exceptions** — including direct messages and private albums. Consent between adults does not apply to these rules because they protect third parties.

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)**. Every uploaded image is matched against perceptual databases (NCMEC, IWF, Project VIC). A match triggers immediate account suspension and a report to the authorities.
- **Non-consensual intimate images (NCII)** and digital gender-based violence as defined by **Argentina's Law 27.736 ("Ley Olimpia")**. We participate in StopNCII.org's perceptual hash database.
- **Impersonation** or use of third-party photos without authorisation.
- **Harassment, threats or hate content** directed at individuals or groups.
- **Promotion of self-harm** (suicide, self-injury, violence against others).
- **Spam, scams, pyramid schemes or "crypto investments"**.
- **Recruitment for illegal activities**, sale of weapons, drugs, or trafficking.

## Other behaviour we limit

- Repeatedly contacting someone who has blocked or ignored you.
- Posting other people's personal information without permission (doxxing).
- Creating multiple accounts or evading suspensions.
- Asking for money or gifts in exchange for intimate content.

## How we enforce these rules

When we receive a report, a member of the moderation team reviews it. Actions range from a warning to temporary or permanent suspension, depending on severity and history. CSAM and NCII trigger immediate suspension and a legal report — there is no first warning.

If your account is suspended you receive an email with the reason and, where applicable, a link to file an appeal.

## How to report

Every profile, message and channel has a Report button. For more information about the process see [Safety](/safety).
